Corporate & Commercial law

Our corporate and commercial practice supports clients from the moment they begin envisioning their business structure. We handle company formation and registration, ensuring that all regulatory requirements with the Corporate Affairs Commission are properly completed. Our corporate and commercial practice supports clients from the moment they begin envisioning their business structure. We handle company formation and registration, ensuring that all regulatory requirements with the Corporate Affairs Commission are properly completed.

We prepare shareholders’ and directors’ agreements that clearly define ownership, responsibilities, governance structures and dispute-resolution mechanisms. For businesses seeking partnerships or expansion, we draft joint venture agreements that protect commercial interests and outline operational frameworks. 

We also manage annual statutory filings and corporate compliance, offering businesses a reliable system that keeps them updated and compliant throughout the year. 
In addition, for clients participating in procurement or government bidding processes, we prepare complete contract-bidding and compliance packages that position them competitively and ensure they meet all legal and regulatory standards.

Intellectual Property Protection

We help individuals and organizations safeguard the creative and commercial assets that define their identity. Our intellectual property services cover trademark searches, filings and registration; copyright advisory and protection; and ongoing portfolio management. We help individuals and organizations safeguard the creative and commercial assets that define their identity. Our intellectual property services cover trademark searches, filings and registration; copyright advisory and protection; and ongoing portfolio management. 

We guide clients through protecting their brand names, logos, written works, digital content, software and other creative outputs, ensuring that their rights are properly secured and enforceable.
 
Beyond registration, we advise on brand strategy, licensing opportunities and infringement issues, helping clients maximize the value of their intellectual property while minimizing legal exposure.

Real Estate Law & Property Advisory

Our real estate practice offers clients clarity and protection at every stage of a property transaction. We conduct thorough title verification and due diligence to confirm ownership status, uncover potential risks and ensure that clients purchase or invest with full knowledge of the property’s legal position.  Our real estate practice offers clients clarity and protection at every stage of a property transaction. We conduct thorough title verification and due diligence to confirm ownership status, uncover potential risks and ensure that clients purchase or invest with full knowledge of the property’s legal position. 

We draft and review real estate agreements including sale contracts, lease agreements, Joint Venture development agreements and investment documentation, ensuring that each transaction is secure and reflects the client’s interests. 

For real estate developers, we provide regulatory compliance guidance, assist with required approvals and ensure adherence to applicable state and federal regulations. Where disputes arise, we offer advisory and representation aimed at achieving practical, timely and cost-effective resolutions.

Employment & HR Advisory

Our employment and HR advisory practice is designed to help organizations create safe, compliant and well-structured workplaces. For companies seeking capacity building, we provide HR and employment-law training sessions for management teams and staff, ensuring that workplace practices align with legal standards and reduce the risk of disputes.
