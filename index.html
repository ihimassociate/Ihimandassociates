Chidera Ihim

Principal Partner
Chidera Ihim is the Principal Partner at Ihim & Associates Legal Consulting, where she advises businesses, property owners, NGOs, and professionals on corporate, real estate and regulatory matters.

She holds an LL.B, BL and an MBA and brings a practical, solutions-driven approach to business law, corporate structuring, and compliance. Her work focuses on helping clients navigate legal risk while building sustainable, well-governed structures.

Chidera is known for her professionalism, Confidentiality and strategic insight, supporting clients to make informed decisions that protect their interests.

Her philosophy:
Strong institutions are built on clarity, structure, and responsible leadership.
